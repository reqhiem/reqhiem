<h2 align="left">Hi 👋! I'm Joel Perca</h2>

PhD student in Applied Mathematics & Data Science at **FGV EMAp**, Rio de Janeiro 🇧🇷 — researching at the **VisualDS Lab** under Jorge Poco.
I work at the intersection of **computer vision**, **urban computing** and **visual analytics**: turning raw video from cities into something people can actually explore and reason about.

- 🔬 Currently working on multi-camera pedestrian tracking, 3D pose reconstruction and visual analytics for urban video
- 🧰 I also run the engineering side of the lab: CI/CD, Docker/Portainer, GPU workstations, private networking
- 🇵🇪 Originally from Puno, Peru (CS @ UNSA) — 🇧🇷 based in Rio de Janeiro
- 🌐 Projects & writing at [reqhiem.dev](https://reqhiem.dev/)
- 📫 Reach me at **joelperca.vc@gmail.com**

<h3 align="left">🚧 What I'm building</h3>

- **UrbanClipAtlas** — foundation-model-driven exploration of large urban video collections (FastAPI · Svelte · D3.js)
- **StreetAware** — multi-camera synchronized pedestrian tracking (YOLO · SAM · DuckDB)
- **3D multi-person reconstruction** — tracking + pose lifting across calibrated camera rigs
